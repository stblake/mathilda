# 258 — parametric A/B of the Trager and Bronstein cascade stages

This benchmark decided which of the Automatic cascade's decision procedures accept integrands
with symbolic parameters, a free symbol other than x such as `a` in `1/Sqrt[x^3 + a]`
(Phase 4.4 step 6, `src/internal/bronstein/BRONSTEIN_PLAN.md`). The setting is
`Integrate`$CascadeParameters`:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `{"Trager"}` | the default: the Trager stage, and Trager on the Kummer pieces inside `ParallelMixedTower` |
| `True` | both stages |
| `{"Bronstein"}` | the Bronstein stage only |
| `False` | neither (the behaviour before v0.6587) |

## Method

- **Corpus.** `corpus.jsonl` holds 1661 parametric integrands, generated by
  `tools/integration_shootout/param_gen.m`. They come from the shootout families
  (`tools/integration_shootout/families.m`, including the parametric families appended at the end),
  the Trager stress families and the Bronstein stress families.
- **Runner.** `tools/integration_shootout/param_ab.py`. Each case runs twice in the same batch:
  A has `$CascadeParameters = False`, and B has the flag under test.
- **Settings and checks.**
  - The timeout is 30 s per arm.
  - An antiderivative is checked by `D[F, x] - f`, evaluated at `a = 1/7` and several x.
  - A certificate (`Integrate::nonelem`) is accepted only if its stage proved it.
- **Gate.**
  - No WRONG, false certificate, disagreement, or lost answer.
  - B's leaf count at most 1.5 × A's + 20.
  - B's time at most 2 × A's + 5 s.
- **Rerun.** The flagged rows are rerun alone (`--rerun`) at lower parallelism. In the first run,
  8 of the 17 flagged rows were timing noise from a loaded machine.

## Results

| Run | B | Better | Worse | Hard failures in B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RESULTS-BOTH.md`, rerun `RERUN-BOTH.md` | `True` | 452 | 124 (110 after the rerun) | 31 (1 after the rerun) |
| `RESULTS-TRAGER.md`, rerun `RERUN-RESULTS-TRAGER.md` | `{"Trager"}` | 239 | 10 (3 after the rerun) | 1 |

- **With `{"Trager"}`**, 239 cases are better:
  - 187 new `Integrate::nonelem` certificates;
  - 28 new antiderivatives;
  - 2 wrong answers corrected (`trager-binom-042`, `trager-binom-045`);
  - the rest smaller or faster.
- **The three confirmed regressions**, open in `tasks/todo.md`:
  - `trager-evensub-005`, `x/(Sqrt[1 + a + x^4] (1 + x^2 + x^4))`: 107 to 280 leaves. This is the
    RootSum presentation over Q(a).
  - `cM-2-67`, `E^x (E^x - 2)/(Sqrt[2 + a + E^x] (1 - E^x + 2 E^(2 x)))`: 78 to 179 leaves. A
    `ParallelMixedTower` Kummer piece is answered by Trager, whose log part over Q(a) is larger.
  - `cA-1-263`: 0.04 to 10 s. A `Together` inside a Trager pre-pass uses its whole budget before
    the fallback.
- **The one hard failure**, `trager-cheb-002` (`x^2 Sqrt[-1 + a + 2 x^4]`), is WRONG in both arms. It
  comes from a stage that is not Trager or Bronstein: a `Sqrt[c] Sqrt[u] -> Sqrt[c u]` merge.
- **With Bronstein on**, most of the regressions are answers 5 to 500 times larger than the ones
  later stages give, because the tower
  presentation over Q(a) is much bigger. So Bronstein stays opt-in.

## Changes made because of the A/B

- **`ParallelMixedTower`.** Before calling Trager on a parametric Kummer piece, it gives its own
  integrator a 3 s first try (`pmtParamFirstBudget`). Trager-first had produced bloated answers
  where that integrator answers compactly. Trying Trager second instead would have lost 12 cases
  where the integrator hangs.
- **The Trager parametric presentation** (`trager/Pipeline.m`, `tragerParamPresent`):
  - log terms are combined;
  - logands are made monic in x;
  - RootSum polynomials are made primitive, and constant log denominators are pulled out by a trace.

  Each tidy is kept only if it is smaller and passes the antiderivative check.

## Files

- `corpus.jsonl`: the 1661 cases.
- `results-both.jsonl`, `RESULTS-BOTH.md`, `rerun-both.jsonl`, `RERUN-BOTH.md`: B = `True`
  (an earlier binary, before the presentation fixes).
- `results-trager.jsonl`, `RESULTS-TRAGER.md`, `rerun-results-trager.jsonl`,
  `RERUN-RESULTS-TRAGER.md`: B = `{"Trager"}`, the final run that set the default.

## Rerunning

```
python3 tools/integration_shootout/param_ab.py --jobs 4 --timeout 30 --b-flag '{"Trager"}' --stem results-trager
python3 tools/integration_shootout/param_ab.py --jobs 3 --timeout 30 --b-flag '{"Trager"}' --stem results-trager --rerun
```

The full run takes about 1.5 h on 4 cores.
