# Changelog: week of 2026-09-07 (Mon) – 2026-09-13 (Sun)

## DSolve M17 — 2nd-order-linear special-function wave (`DSolve`SpecialFunctionForm`)

Two verified increments on the largest §2.1.2 corpus bucket (2nd-order linear),
both emitting heads that numericize (`Hypergeometric2F1`, `BesselJ/Y/I/K`,
`AiryAi/Bi`) so both the in-method numeric self-verify **and** the corpus's numeric
back-substitution are genuine — the 0-FAIL corpus invariant is preserved. All in
`src/calculus/dsolve_specialform.c`; no new method file or cascade edit.

- **Affine + local-exponent reduction to Gauss ₂F₁.** Any rational-coefficient
  hypergeometric-class equation with exactly two finite regular singular points
  `{x1, x2}` (the poles of `P`, `Q`; a squarefree `PolynomialLCM` of degree 2) is
  mapped affinely `x = x1 + h s` onto the canonical interval `x(1-x)`, then the
  local exponents are pulled out by an **F-homotopy** `Y = s^r0 (1-s)^r1 F` (the
  indicial roots at `s=0,1`) so the reduced `F`-equation is canonical Gauss and
  solves as `Hypergeometric2F1`. This reaches **Gegenbauer, Jacobi, and associated
  Legendre at symbolic (non-integer) degree/order** — exactly the cases Kovacic
  declines (no Liouvillian solution) — as verifiable 2F1 closed forms. Gated by a
  numeric self-verify (`hgc_num_ok`) that samples inside the mapped interval, the
  sole guard against a mis-mapped 2F1 (whose residual `zero_test` cannot disprove).
  The canonical Gauss row was factored into `specialform_gauss_basis` and reused.
- **Ordinary vs associated Legendre.** The Legendre recognizer now emits `LegendreP`
  only for the ordinary case (`mu == 0`, which numericizes); the associated case
  (`mu != 0`) declines to the affine/F-homotopy row, since a 3-argument
  `LegendreP[nu, mu, x]` does not numericize for symbolic `nu, mu` (its residual
  verify and the corpus check both fail).
- **Liouville normal-form pre-pass.** An equation carrying a `y'` term is also
  tried through its normal form `z'' = r z` (`y = z Exp[-Int P/2]`, when the
  integral is elementary) against the y'-free Airy/Bessel recognisers (factored into
  `specialform_reduced_basis`); a match multiplies the recovered basis by the
  recovery factor. Gated by `sf_num_ok`.
- **Robustness.** Both the mapped coefficients and the indicial-limit extraction use
  `Cancel[Together[.]]` rather than `Simplify` (the rational-in-`s` reduction actually
  needed; `Simplify` blows up trying to canonicalise the radical-parametric constants).
  Four decline-fast gates keep the recognizer from hanging on equations it cannot close
  (all fall through to the cascade / series fallback): (1) the indicial `FactorList`/
  `Solve` quadratic is skipped above 80 leaves (a many-parameter equation gives radical
  `a+b`, `ab` that `Solve` grinds on); (2) the affine row runs only when the two regular
  singular points are RATIONAL (radical RSPs — roots of an irreducible quadratic leading
  coefficient — are not closed here and make every downstream symbolic step slow);
  (3) the normal-form pre-pass is skipped when the potential `-r` exceeds 50 leaves; and
  (4) the pre-pass runs at top level only (`g_dsolve_depth <= 1`) — a recursive caller
  such as `OperatorFactor` re-solving an order-(n-1) quotient could otherwise compose the
  `mu = Exp[-Int P/2]` recovery factor into an infinite rewrite (`$IterationLimit`).

## Factor / Simplify — dropped leading term on unexpanded `Power[Plus[...], n]`

`Factor` (and `FactorList`, and `Simplify`/`FullSimplify`, which call `Factor`) returned
a WRONG, lower-degree result when the argument contained an unexpanded squared linear
form with numeric coefficients: `Factor[(5-3s)^2 - 30 s]` gave `-5(-5+12s)` (dropping the
`9 s^2` term) instead of the irreducible `9 s^2 - 60 s + 25`. Root cause:
`flint_univariate_factor` (`src/poly/flint_bridge.c`) computed the degree via
`get_degree_poly` on the RAW argument, and `get_degree_poly` counts `Power[base,k]`
toward the degree only when `base` IS the variable — so `(5-3s)^2` reported degree 0,
capping the total degree at 1 and never querying the `s^2` coefficient. Fix:
`flint_univariate_factor` now `expr_expand`s once before computing the degree and
extracting coefficients (`get_degree_poly`'s "input must be expanded" precondition is now
documented in `src/poly/poly.c`). Regression:
`tests/test_factor_baseline.c::test_unexpanded_power_input`.

**Coverage.** Solves the corpus trig-potential associated-Legendre family (e.g.
`(p(1+p) - k^2 Csc^2 x) y + Cot x y' + y'' == 0`, via M14's `t = Cos x` reduction
into a rational associated-Legendre equation and then this recognizer), Gegenbauer/
Jacobi at symbolic degree, shifted-interval Gauss equations, and Bessel/Airy
equations with a first-derivative term. Still declines: integer 2F1 lower-parameter
cases (dependent solutions), two-power/Heun potentials, the quartic
parabolic-cylinder potential (no native `ParabolicCylinderD`), and three-finite-RSP
Heun/Möbius equations.

Tests: `tests/test_dsolve.c` (`t_m17_*`), the forward-generator stress suite

normal-form-Bessel grids, all back-substitution verified), and the §2.1.2 corpus
dashboard (`dsolve_corpus_2_1_2_tests`). `make check-c99` green; refactor
